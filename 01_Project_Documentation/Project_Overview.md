# 6M Mart – Retail Intelligence & Performance Analytics

## 1. Project Overview

6M Mart is an end-to-end Business Intelligence portfolio project designed around a fictional Philippine supermarket chain operating eight branches.

The project demonstrates how data analytics and business intelligence tools can transform raw retail data into meaningful business insights. It covers the complete analytical workflow, from synthetic data generation and data preparation to database management, SQL analysis, and interactive Power BI dashboards.

**Important:** All datasets used in this project are synthetically generated for educational and portfolio demonstration purposes. They do not represent actual business transactions or customer records.

## 2. Business Scenario

6M Mart operates eight supermarket branches across Cavite and Metro Manila. Management requires a centralized reporting solution to monitor business performance, identify operational concerns, and support data-driven decision-making.

The project approaches this scenario from the perspective of a Business Intelligence Analyst reporting to an Operations Director.

## 3. Project Objectives

- Analyze overall sales and profitability trends.
- Compare branch-level sales and operational performance.
- Identify high-performing and underperforming products.
- Evaluate promotional sales and profitability.
- Monitor inventory availability, stockouts, and overstock conditions.
- Analyze product returns and refund activity.
- Identify business opportunities and provide actionable recommendations.

## 4. Dataset Overview

The project covers January 1, 2024, through December 31, 2025.

| Dataset | Records |
|---|---:|
| Branches | 8 |
| Suppliers | 15 |
| Products | 120 |
| Promotions | 15 |
| Date dimension | 731 |
| Sales line items | 120,000 |
| Inventory records | 101,760 |
| Return records | 2,469 |
| **Total** | **225,118** |

The database consists of eight interconnected tables, organized into dimension and fact tables.

## 5. Technology Stack

- **Python:** Synthetic data generation, data cleaning, and validation.
- **Pandas and NumPy:** Data manipulation and processing.
- **PostgreSQL:** Relational database management and data storage.
- **SQL:** Business analysis, KPI calculations, and insight generation.
- **Power BI:** Interactive dashboards and business reporting.
- **DAX:** Measures, calculated columns, and time-based comparisons.
- **Microsoft Excel:** Supporting data review and validation.

## 6. Analytical Scope

The project contains seven Power BI report pages:

1. Executive Overview

3. Product Performance

6. Returns & Refunds Analysis

This project demonstrates practical skills in data preparation, relational database design, SQL analysis, KPI development, dashboard creation, and communicating business insights.

It is intended as a portfolio demonstration of an end-to-end Business Intelligence workflow.

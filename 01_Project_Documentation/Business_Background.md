# 6M Mart — Business Background

## 1. Company Overview

6M Mart is a fictional Philippine supermarket chain operating eight physical retail branches across Cavite and Metro Manila.

The company provides everyday consumer necessities, including groceries, beverages, dairy products, fresh produce, personal care products, and household essentials.

The business operates through in-store transactions and periodic promotional campaigns.

As the company expands, management seeks to improve its understanding of business performance through data analytics and business intelligence.

## 5. Business Operations

6M Mart processes daily in-store customer purchases through its point-of-sale (POS) system.

Transactions contain product information, quantities, prices, discounts, transaction dates, and branch identifiers.

Inventory quantities are updated based on product purchases. The company also conducts promotional campaigns to encourage customer purchases and improve sales performance.

Management monitors branch performance, sales, product profitability, promotional effectiveness, and inventory availability.

## 6. Business Challenges

Management wants better visibility into the following areas:

- Differences in sales performance across branches.
- Monthly and yearly sales trends.
- Product revenue and profitability.
- Effectiveness of promotional campaigns.
- Inventory availability and potential stock issues.
- Branches experiencing declining performance.

## 7. Project Objective

The objective of this Business Intelligence project is to develop an end-to-end analytics solution that transforms simulated retail data into actionable business insights.

The project aims to evaluate sales performance, identify profitability opportunities, assess promotional effectiveness, investigate inventory-related problems, and provide data-driven recommendations to support management decisions.

## 8. Data Scope and Limitations

This project uses simulated data representing a fictional supermarket business.

All transactions, products, suppliers, inventory records, and business events will be synthetically generated.

The findings and recommendations will demonstrate analytical methods and business intelligence capabilities rather than represent the actual performance of a real company.
